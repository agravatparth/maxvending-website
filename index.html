Home / About

Three and a half decades of perfect vending.

Max Vending is an Indian company that designs and manufactures beverage vending machines, and has been doing it for more than 36 years.

We design and build our machines in India, so we control the quality of every part and stand behind every cup.

What started as a small team of engineers has grown into one of the established names in Indian beverage vending. Our machines pour tea and coffee in offices, factories, hospitals, institutions and shops.

We work from a modern facility with up-to-date equipment and machinery, which lets us keep quality high and prices fair. Our range now runs from the compact Cute to recipe-mode Infinity machines and bean-to-cup coffee.

Every premix machine is BIS certified and comes with temperature interlocking, digital counters and auto cleaning as standard.
